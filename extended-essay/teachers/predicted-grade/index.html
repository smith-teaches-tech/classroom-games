For Teachers

Predicted grade

You don't mark the essay. You read the final version and choose the grade description that fits it best.

  1. Read the final essay before the viva voce.
  2. Compare the essay with the grades below. Choose the best fit; the essay doesn't need to match every point.
  3. Send the grade (A to E) to the EE coordinator by .

E is a failing grade. A student needs a D or higher in the EE to be awarded the diploma. If you expect an E, tell the EE coordinator early. (guide p. 14)
