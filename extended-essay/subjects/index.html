Subject guides

What the IB expects from an Extended Essay in each subject you can choose at ASD. Read these subject guides before you choose your subject.

Your subject choice and one backup subject are due on the date in the EE calendar. Read the subject guide for your first choice and for your backup. Then use the thinking tool at the bottom of each subject guide to test your idea.

Your subject from step 1 is highlighted.

Studies in language and literature

Language A

Close analysis of a literary or language text, supported by literary criticism.

Language acquisition

Language B

The language, culture or literature of the language you're learning, studied through texts first written in that language.

Individuals and societies

Business Management

A real organisation or business issue, analysed with business management tools and theory.

Economics

An economic theory tested against real evidence, often in one place and time period.

Global Politics

A political issue or case, analysed through the main political concepts and academic sources.

Sciences

Biology

A biology experiment (or similar published data) with controlled variables and a standardised method.

Chemistry

A chemistry investigation (or similar published data) with controlled variables and a standardised method.

Physics

A physics investigation (or similar published data) with controlled variables and a standardised method.

Mathematics

Mathematics

Mathematics beyond the syllabus, or mathematics used to model or analyse data. A literature review is required.

The arts

Visual Arts

Analysis of a small body of artworks in their context, supported by art history and criticism. Images are required.

Two subjects

Interdisciplinary EE

Two DP subjects and one of the five frameworks, working on one research question.

Writing your EE in another subject? Ask the EE coordinator first, then read that subject's section of the IB Extended essay guide. The sample essays include History, Psychology, ESS and Design Technology.
