For Students

Your EE, step by step

Nineteen steps take you from choosing a subject to your viva voce (your final reflection session with your supervisor). Do the steps in order. Each step has a short guide and a private thinking tool to help you develop your ideas.

New here? Begin with Start here. It explains how the steps work and how to set up your RRS. Your RRS (researcher's reflection space) is your own EE notebook.
