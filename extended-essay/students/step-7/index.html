Classroom tools/Extended Essay/For Students/Step 7

Collect 40 sources in PowerNotes

Bring your reading together: 40 sources, organised so you can see what your topic is really about.

Why this step matters

Forty sources is more than you will cite — and that is the point. A wide collection shows you the shape of your topic: the themes that keep returning, the arguments that matter, and the gaps. In step 9 you will narrow these 40 to the 20 best.

Organise PowerNotes

You already have four categories from steps 3 to 6. Keep them, and add theme categories as patterns appear.

Open / background

From step 3. Orientation and key terms.

Current

From step 4. Recent research and debate.

Historical

From step 5. Foundational work.

Methods

From step 6. How your question could be answered.

For every source, check that PowerNotes has a full citation in your chosen style, and add a one-line note on why the source matters.

Check your collection

  • Spread: no single category should hold more than about half your sources.
  • Quality: a good share of your sources should be scholarly — peer-reviewed articles, academic books, or data from reputable organisations.
  • Perspectives: do your sources disagree with each other anywhere? If every source says the same thing, look for the other side.
  • Records: every source has a complete citation in your chosen style.

Then step back

Read your one-line notes from top to bottom. Which themes keep coming up? Which sources do you keep going back to? Where do you feel most curious? Those answers point to the narrower topic you will choose in step 8.
