Classroom tools/Extended Essay/For Students/Step 2

Choose your citation style: APA or MLA

Decide now how you'll cite sources, before you collect a single one. Then you'll never have to redo your records.

Why this step matters

From step 3 onward you'll collect between 10 and 25 sources, depending on your subject (step 7 gives the number for your subject). If you record each source in your chosen style as you find it, your works cited list at step 17 will be almost finished. If you switch styles halfway, you'll have to redo every entry.

The IB doesn't require one particular style. It does require you to acknowledge (give credit to) every source using a standard referencing style, and to use that style consistently throughout the essay. At ASD you'll use APA or MLA.

The table shows the usual choice for each subject. Confirm your style with your supervisor, then use it for the whole essay. For an interdisciplinary essay, choose the style that suits the subject doing most of the work, and agree on it with your supervisor.

Use the current edition of your style. At the time of writing, that's the MLA Handbook (9th edition) and the APA Publication Manual (7th edition). The Purdue Online Writing Lab (Purdue OWL) has clear guides to both: MLA guide · APA guide.

Works cited, reference list and bibliography

  • A reference list (APA) includes only the sources you cite in the body of your essay. In MLA, the same list is called works cited.
  • A bibliography lists every source you read or used during your research, including the ones you didn't cite.

What to record for every source

Whatever style you choose, write down these details as soon as you find a source. They're much harder to find again later:

  • Author(s) or organisation
  • Title of the work (and the journal, book or website it appears in)
  • Date of publication
  • Publisher
  • URL or DOI, and the date you accessed it
  • Page numbers for anything you might quote

Set up PowerNotes

  1. Log in to PowerNotes and create a project for your extended essay.
  2. Set the project's citation style to the style you have chosen, if your account offers that setting.
  3. Every time you save a source from step 3 onward, check the citation PowerNotes creates. Automatic citations often miss authors, dates or page numbers. Fix any mistakes straight away.
