Sample essays

Real IB example essays, sorted by subject. Each one shows the grade it earned, the examiner's marks for each criterion, and — where we have it — the student's Reflection and Progress Form (RPF).

About the grades. The IB gives every essay a mark out of 30, and the letter grade (A–E) comes from where that mark falls. The IB has not published official 2027 grade boundaries yet, so every letter on this page is an estimate. Read the examiner's comments — they explain the grade far better than the letter does.

Estimated boundaries (out of 30): A 24–30 · B 19–23 · C 14–18 · D 7–13 · E 0–6. A few older essays were marked out of 34 on the previous rubric (A 27–34 · B 21–26 · C 14–20 · D 7–13 · E 0–6); the criteria have changed since, but what makes a strong essay has not.
