Round One

Place It: The Gaps Are Marked

Each box marks a spot that needs a decision. Tap a box, choose a mark (or none), then press Check. Some boxes accept more than one mark, and the feedback tells you what each choice does. After a correct answer, name the rule: the test asks you to justify every dash, semicolon and colon.

Round Two

Find the Gap: No Hints

This round works like Part 1 of the test: you decide where the marks go. Tap the space between any two words to add a mark there. Marks that are already printed are correct and stay put.

Round Three

Fix It: Repair the Errors

Every sentence has at least one punctuation error with a colon, semicolon or dash. Tap a mark to change it, or choose none to delete it. Some marks are already right: changing those will cost you.

Round Four

Combine: Build One Sentence

Combine each set into one sentence using at least one dash, semicolon or colon. The pieces are already in order; you may add words like and, but or while. Check your sentence against the checklist, then compare it with a model answer. Your sentence can be different from the model and still be right.

Your Paragraph

Write: Your Argument, Your Marks

Write a 150–200 word argument paragraph about a school issue you care about. Then label every dash, semicolon and colon with the rule it follows, exactly as you will on the test.
