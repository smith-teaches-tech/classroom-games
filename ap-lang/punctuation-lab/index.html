The ChatGPT Dash

AI writing tools use dashes, semicolons and colons constantly, so often that some readers now call the em dash “the ChatGPT dash.” A chatbot can put a mark in the right place without being able to explain why it belongs there. By the end of this lesson, you will be able to do both: place each mark and explain it.

The volume dial

Commas, parentheses and dashes can all hold the same extra information. What changes is the volume. Pick a sentence, then turn the dial.

How this lesson works. For each mark, you will study real sentences first and write the rule in your own words. Then you check your rule against the real one. Dashes have three jobs, semicolons have three jobs, and colons have one rule with two tests.

Typing an em dash

Mac: Option + Shift + hyphen.   Windows: hold Alt and type 0151 on the number pad.   Chromebook: Ctrl + Shift + U, type 2014, then press Enter. No spaces on either side of the dash.
