Case Builder

KQHow do historians construct a narrative from incomplete traces — and what would it take to know they had got it right?
The rule for every case: anything you say happened, point to a card. Anything you worked out, say “we infer.” Nobody in this group saw Friday night. All you have is what survived.

Evidence file · 26 cards

Click into any part of your case, then press Cite on a card to add its number there. Press Note to write on a card: do you trust it? does it clash with another card?

Group notes

Rough working: questions, theories, things to check. Notes on a single card go on the card itself.

1 · Our claim

One sentence: who killed John Doe, how, and why.

2 · What happened, in order

One step per event, in time order. For each step: does a card show it, or do we infer it?

3 · Why

The motive. What reason did this person have, and which cards point to it?

5 · What we can’t explain

Be honest. A case that admits its gaps is easier to trust than one that pretends it has none.

The last sentence the room will hear. Why is your account better supported than the others?
