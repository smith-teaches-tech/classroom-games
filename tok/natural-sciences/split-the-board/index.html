One more thing

Your board had seven rules. But nobody said the real rule had to be one of seven. Here are rules that were never on your board. Each one gives exactly the same answers as the hidden rule on every triple you tested.

A bit like 20 Questions

Good 20 Questions players don't ask "Is it a cat?" They ask "Is it alive?", a question that splits what's left roughly in half, so either answer teaches them something. That is what a good test does.

But there is one big difference. In 20 Questions, you know the answer is somewhere in the game. In science, the real answer might not be on anyone's board.
