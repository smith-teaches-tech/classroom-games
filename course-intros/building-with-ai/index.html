Let’s start with you
Do your children use AI?
Do you know what they’re using it for?
Mostly two ways: to help write essays — often blurring the line between honest and dishonest — and to study, asking it to explain things. That second one is fine, but narrow.
What actually makes AI transformative is building — custom software and tools made for your own needs. That’s what this course teaches: how to build, and how the apps work underneath.
Presenter
Ask the two questions for real; let silence sit. Then reveal. Land it: most kids only ever reach the narrow uses on their own. This course takes them to the powerful one.
What this course is

Students design and build real web apps that run live on the internet — working with AI as their development partner.

The AI writes the code. Your child designs it, directs it, and must be able to explain it — finishing with a portfolio of working apps that are theirs from idea to live URL.

Presenter
The reframe: not “let the robot do the homework.” It’s learning to architect and direct software. AI is the tool; judgment is the skill.
The part most people never see

They learn the plumbing

A working app is more than a screen. Your child learns what’s behind it — where data lives, how sign-in works, how the pieces connect.

Presenter
“The plumbing” is the whole point: anyone can prompt an app into existence, but understanding the pipes is what makes it real learning — and what we grade.

The question every parent asks

“Isn’t this just letting AI do the work?”

Here, using AI isn’t just allowed — it’s required. So there’s nothing to hide: everyone uses it, out in the open.
That flips the question from “did you use AI?” to “do you understand what you built?” Understanding is the graded skill — and the one the working world now wants.
Presenter
Honest selling point: because AI is required, I’m not policing secret use. Everyone’s using it — so the bar becomes understanding, which is the real skill.
How your child is graded — it’s this simple

Two Questions

Does the app work?
✓
Can they explain how it works?
✓
Both yes
A

A live app they can explain, step by step, earns top marks. No final exam.

Presenter
This is the whole grading philosophy in two questions. “Explain how it works” is checked with a short talk-through and a hand-drawn diagram of the app — that’s what AI can’t fake.
