What this course is

Students design and build real web apps that run live on the internet — working with AI as their development partner.

The AI writes the code. Your child designs it, directs it, and must be able to explain it. They finish with a portfolio of original, working apps that are theirs from idea to live URL.

Presenter
The key reframe for parents: this is not “let the robot do your homework.” It’s learning to architect and direct software. AI is the tool; judgment is the skill.

How your child is graded

The “Ship-Gate”

Because AI writes the code, grading targets what AI can’t fake. Every project must pass four checks:

1
It works
A live URL, with that unit’s system actually functioning.
2
Explanation — handwritten
They explain, step by step, how their app works.
3
Data-flow diagram — handwritten
Boxes & arrows: where data goes, where secrets live, who sees what.
4
Testing check
Evidence it works on a phone, for someone who isn’t them, under a few attempts to break it.

The grade is the four builds plus a final Showcase. Design and usability count — and there is no final exam.

Presenter
This is the heart of the course’s integrity. The handwritten explanation and diagram are the anti-cheat: an app you can’t explain doesn’t pass, no matter how polished.
The question every parent asks

“Isn’t this just letting AI do the work?”

Using AI to write code is the point of the course. But an app your child can’t explain doesn’t pass the gate.
The graded skill is understanding — how the pieces fit, where data flows, why it works. That’s exactly the skill the working world is now asking for.

Misuse or unethical use of these tools is taken seriously and will be reported to the school.

Presenter
Meet the concern head-on. The course is designed so that using AI without understanding fails. Understanding & direction are what’s assessed.
