Grading

This course is ungraded. Instead of chasing points, students receive detailed feedback on their work.
A student who submits assessments on time, integrates that feedback over the semester, and shows real improvement will be awarded an A or B at the end of each semester.
An A is awarded when a student’s achievement aligns with a score of 4 or 5 on the AP Language exam.
Presenter
Emphasise the shift: feedback over points lowers the fear of failure and raises the quality of writing. Improvement is the metric.
Setting expectations honestly

The Time It Takes

This is a college-level English class. A reasonable rule for college work is about two hours outside of class for every hour spent in it.
I’ll do my best to keep homework manageable and to avoid stacking multiple major assessments in the same week. Even so, there will be some stressful weeks — and that’s normal.
Presenter
Be honest about workload so it isn’t a surprise in November. Reassure that you actively manage the calendar.
