Rubric (out of 30)

Click a mark; open “descriptors” for band guidance. Note: the text shown is a short summary — full IB band descriptors (0 / 1–2 / 3–4 …) to be loaded from the official 2027 guide.
A · Framework for the essay/6
Strands: research question · research methods · structure.
5–6: sharply focused RQ; well-chosen methods applied effectively; structure fully supports the argument.
3–4: appropriate RQ and methods; structure mostly sound, some lapses.
1–2: broad/descriptive RQ; limited or ill-suited methods; weak structure.
0: below the standard above.
Paraphrased — confirm against the official 2027 guide. Absorbs the old “Presentation” criterion.
B · Knowledge and understanding/6
Strands: knowledge · terminology · concepts.
5–6: strong, consistent subject knowledge; terminology and concepts accurate and effectively applied.
3–4: adequate knowledge; terminology/concepts mostly accurate.
1–2: limited knowledge; inconsistent terminology.
0: below the standard above.
Paraphrased — confirm against the official 2027 guide.
C · Analysis and line of argument/6
Strands: analysis · line of argument.
5–6: insightful analysis of findings; coherent, well-developed line of argument throughout.
3–4: some analysis; argument present but uneven.
1–2: largely descriptive; little clear argument.
0: below the standard above.
Paraphrased — confirm against the official 2027 guide.
D · Discussion and evaluation/8highest weight
Strands: discussion · evaluation.
7–8: sustained discussion; evidence weighed and sources evaluated throughout the essay, not just the conclusion.
5–6: good discussion/evaluation, not fully sustained.
3–4: some evaluation, mostly toward the end.
1–2: minimal evaluation.
0: below the standard above.
Paraphrased — confirm against the official 2027 guide. Highest-weighted criterion (8 marks).
E · Reflection/4from the RPF
Assessed from the RPF (≤500 words after the viva).
4: genuine growth as a learner; clear, specific transferable (ATL) skills.
2–3: some growth and skills development shown.
1: superficial or procedural reflection.
0: below the standard above.
Paraphrased — confirm against the official 2027 guide.
