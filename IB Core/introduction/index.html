Presenter · the hidden map
The five zones quietly seed the whole course. 1&2 → the knower (TOK). 3 → the contributor (CAS). 4&5 → the inquirer (EE). Don't reveal this until the end. Press T for a work timer.
Start here · the centre

Your name, and a symbol that represents you.

A drawing, an object, an animal, a shape — something that feels like you at a glance.

Zone 1

The people, places, and communities that made me who I am.

Family, friends, a team, a country, a faith, a corner of the internet — who and what shaped you?

Seeds
Knower / TOK. Where perspective comes from. Bank anything students say here — you'll call back to it when TOK opens.
Zone 2

Things I'm pretty sure are true — about the world, or about life.

Big or small. What do you feel certain about?

Seeds
Knower / TOK. This is the setup for the class-5 destabiliser: “where did that certainty actually come from?” Don't spring it yet.
Zone 3

Something in the world I care about, or wish were different.

A cause, a problem, a person or place you'd want to help. Something that actually moves you.

Seeds
Contributor / CAS. This is your CAS on-ramp for next class — students are surfacing experience ideas without realising it.
Zone 4

What I'm good at, and how I do my best work.

A skill, a strength, a way of working. Alone or with people? Hands-on or in your head?

Seeds
Inquirer / EE (and CAS). Strengths and working style — the raw material of a good research process.
Zone 5

A question I've always wondered about.

Anything. Silly or serious. Something you've never had a real answer to.

Seeds
Inquirer / EE. Somewhere down this road is a 4,000-word question. Note the good ones.
