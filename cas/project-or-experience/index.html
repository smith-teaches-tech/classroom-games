CAS · The CAS Project

Project or Experience?

Most of what you log for CAS will be experiences. At least one thing has to be a CAS project. Today you learn to tell the two apart, so the project you plan actually counts.

By the end of today: I can name the five CAS stages, use the four tests to decide whether something is a CAS project or a CAS experience, and start testing my own project ideas.

01 · THE FIVE CAS STAGES

Every CAS project moves through five stages

The CAS stages are the plan for doing CAS well. A one-off experience might skip some of them. A CAS project has to use all five.

Stage 1Investigation

Work out what you want to do and why. Look at your interests and skills, and decide the purpose and goals.

What do I want to achieve, and why does it matter?

Service: find a real need, and check it with the people you want to help.

Stage 2Preparation

Make the plan. Decide who does which job, what you need, and the timeline. Learn any skills you are missing.

Who is doing what, by when, and with what?

Stage 3Action

Carry out the plan. Things will go wrong, so you will need to make decisions and solve problems along the way.

What do we do when the plan stops working?

Stage 4Reflection

Describe what happened, how you felt, and what you are learning. Reflection happens all the way through the CAS project, not only at the end.

What happened, what did I learn, and what should I change?

Stage 5Demonstration

Show what you did and what you learned. You can share it in your CAS portfolio or with an audience.

How will I show other people what we did and what I learned?

Reflection can send you back to Investigation or Preparation. The stages are a cycle, not a checklist you do once.

02 · EXPERIENCE OR PROJECT?

Four tests that only a CAS project passes

CAS experience

Anything you do in Creativity, Activity or Service. It can be a single event, or something you do many times. Most of your CAS will be experiences, and they all count.

CAS project

A planned set of CAS experiences, done with other people, over at least a month. Every CAS student must complete at least one CAS project.

A CAS project has to pass all four tests. If it fails even one test, it is still a CAS experience. It is just not a CAS project.

TEST 1

Team

You work with other students or community members, and everyone contributes.

TEST 2

One month+

From the first planning to the finish, it lasts at least one month.

TEST 3

All five stages

It goes through Investigation, Preparation, Action, Reflection and Demonstration, starting with a clear purpose and goals.

TEST 4

Ownership

You started it, or you are responsible for part of it. Just turning up is not enough.

Service projects: Investigation must find a real need. Ask the people you are helping what they need. Do not guess for them. A service project built on a guess fails Test 3.
