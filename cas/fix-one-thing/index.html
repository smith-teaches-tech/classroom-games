Before you go — what to look for

You have walked these corridors for years and stopped seeing them. Each question below finds a different kind of thing.

  1. Where do people slow down, hesitate, or look around? Confusion has body language. Watch for it.
  2. What has someone already tried to fix with tape, a handwritten sign, or a propped-open door? Someone got there before you — and it didn't hold. Why not?
  3. What do you end up explaining to new students every year? If it needs explaining every September, the building isn't saying it.
  4. What would you notice if today were your first day here?
