Break the Lock

Building with AI · Admins, moderators and Row Level Security

Hiding a button is not a lock. Anyone can send a request to your database from the browser console. The RLS rules in Supabase are the only thing that decides what happens.

1Meet the test accounts

These are fake accounts. You'll make the same kind of accounts in your own app so you can test it properly.

The dashboard is different. When you use the Supabase dashboard, you're the app owner. RLS doesn't apply to you there. That's why the dashboard is the only safe place to make someone an admin.

5Now do it to your own app

  1. Make the test accounts in your own app: one for your admin, one for a moderator and two normal users. If Confirm email is turned off in your Supabase sign-in settings, made-up emails like sam.test@example.com will work.
  2. In the Supabase dashboard, put your admin account in the admins table by hand. This is the only way anyone becomes an admin.
  3. Sign in as your admin and use your admin panel to make the moderator account a moderator.
  4. Sign in as each test account and try every test from Part 4 in your real app. Use the console for the ones the app has no button for. Ask Gemini to write the console line for your app.
  5. Write down every result: what you tried, who you were signed in as, and what happened.
A blocked request often looks like nothing happened. Adding a row gives you a red RLS error. Changing or deleting a row you're not allowed to touch gives no error at all. It just changes zero rows. Always check the table afterward.
