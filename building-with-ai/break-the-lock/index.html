Hiding a button is not a lock. The RLS rules in Supabase are the lock. Every request is checked by them, whether it comes from your app or from someone typing in the console.

2A rule opens the whole row

Your app needs this rule so people can change their username: "Users can update their own row in profiles." RLS can't pick out single columns. If you can update a row, you can change every cell in it. So where you keep someone's role matters.

4Test your own app with fake accounts

Now do this for real. You need one account for each kind of person, so you can sign in as each one and try to break your app.

  1. Make four test accounts in your app: an admin, a moderator and two normal users. If Confirm email is turned off in your Supabase sign-in settings, made-up emails like sam.test@example.com will work.
  2. In the Supabase dashboard, add your admin account to the admins table by hand. The dashboard skips RLS, so it's the only safe place to make an admin.
  3. Sign in as your admin. Use your admin panel to make the moderator account a moderator.
  4. Sign in as each account and try everything below. Use the console for anything your app has no button for. Ask Gemini to write the console line for your app.
